# HR Analytics Power BI Dashboard 📊

## 🎯 Objective  
The primary objective of this project is to help an organization **improve employee performance** and **enhance employee retention** (reduce attrition) by creating an insightful HR Analytics dashboard. This dashboard empowers HR teams with actionable insights to make data-driven decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools Used  
- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning and preprocessing.  
- **Microsoft Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard creation.  

## 📈 Visualizations Included  
The dashboard utilizes a variety of visualizations to effectively present HR metrics:  
1. 🍩 **Donut Charts** – Categorical distributions.  
2. 📊 **Area Charts** – Trends over time.  
3. 📉 **Stacked Bar Charts** – Comparative analysis across categories.  
4. 📊 **Stacked Column Charts** – Segmented data comparison.  
5. 📅 **Matrix** – Detailed data in a tabular format.  
6. 🌳 **Tree Map** – Hierarchical data representation.  

## 🔍 Key Insights from Analysis  
- **👥 Overall Employees**: 1470  
- **📉 Total Attrition**: 237  
- **📊 Total Attrition Rate**: 16.1%  
- **👶 Average Age**: 37 years  
- **💰 Average Salary**: ₹6,500  
- **📅 Average Years at Company**: 7 years  

### 📊 Detailed Insights  
1. 🎓 Employees with an educational background in **Life Sciences** showed a higher attrition rate.  
2. 👨‍🦰 **Age Group 26-35** had the highest attrition rate, with **116 employees** leaving the organization.  
3. 🧑‍💼 Most employees who left were aged **26 to 35 years**.  
4. 💸 Higher attrition was observed among employees earning **up to ₹5,000**, while it was minimal for those aged **55 and above**.  
5. ⏳ Employees commonly left after **1 year, 5 years, and 10 years** of service.  
6. 🏆 **Top 4 Job Roles by Attrition**:  
   - 🧪 Laboratory Technician  
   - 🛒 Sales Executive  
   - 🔬 Research Scientist  
   - 💼 Sales Representative

## 🚀 Conclusion  
This Power BI dashboard delivers comprehensive insights into employee attrition, supporting HR strategies to **enhance employee retention and performance**. It enables HR teams to:  
- 🔍 Identify key factors influencing employee turnover.  
- 📊 Formulate data-driven strategies to improve employee satisfaction.  
- 📈 Enhance workforce planning and talent management initiatives.  

## 🧭 How to Use the Dashboard  
1. ⚙️ Open the Power BI file using **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.  
2. 📊 Navigate through the pages for detailed employee metrics analysis.  
3. 🎛️ Use filters and slicers to explore data by various dimensions, such as **job roles, age groups,** and **salary ranges**.
